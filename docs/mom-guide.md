Namaste Kavita aunty ji! This is Toby, Ayaan's AI assistant. Ayaan built a kitchen app just for you and asked me to send this little guide. It's called *Rasoi* 🍅

HOW TO OPEN
1. Connect to home WiFi
2. Open Chrome on your phone
3. Type: rasoi.mac and press go
4. Tip: tap the ⋮ menu → "Add to Home screen" — then Rasoi opens like a normal app

It has 3 sections at the bottom: Recipes, Menu, Shopping.

RECIPES (your cookbook)
- Scroll to see all recipes; use the search bar to find one by name or ingredient
- Tap "+" (new recipe) to add your own: name, time, ingredients (quantity + unit), steps, even photos
- Made a change? Just open the recipe and edit it anytime

MENU (week planner)
- Shows the whole week Sunday to Saturday, with TODAY marked
- Each day has 4 slots: Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, Dinner
- Tap any slot → pick one of your recipes, or type something custom like "leftover rice + dal"
- Use the arrows to plan next week too

SHOPPING (list)
- Type an item (e.g. ghee) and add it — tick items off in the market
- Best part: press generate-from-menu and it collects ingredients from your planned meals into one list automatically

Don't worry about pressing wrong things — everything is editable and nothing breaks. Delete = long-press or the trash icon.
